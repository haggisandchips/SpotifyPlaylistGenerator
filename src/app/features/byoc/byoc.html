Why is this happening?

This app is registered with Spotify in "Development Mode", which caps it at 5 manually-approved Spotify accounts. That's a Spotify platform restriction, not a choice made by this project.

Small, non-commercial hobby apps like this one aren't eligible to move out of Development Mode, so this is the only way for anyone outside that allowlist to use it.

Creating your own free Spotify app takes a couple of minutes, and its Client ID is stored only in your browser.
